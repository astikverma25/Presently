Still Using Registers for Attendance?

Manual attendance registers take time, create errors, and make it difficult to track student records accurately. A digital attendance app helps schools save time, reduce paperwork, and manage attendance in real time.

Time-Consuming Process

Teachers spend valuable class time marking attendance manually every day.

High Chance of Errors

Paper rolls and proxy marking lead to inaccurate records and compliance issues.

No Real-Time Updates

Admins and guardians must wait until day end or weeks for attendance tallies.

Difficult Monthly Reports

Compiling semester averages and eligibility lists takes days of manual calculation.

Paperwork Overload

Physical registers get misplaced, damaged, and require tedious physical storage.

Limited Attendance Insights

No actionable trend alerts to identify chronic student absenteeism before exams.

How Digital Attendance Works?

A simple step-by-step process to mark attendance helps schools save time, reduce errors, and manage student records in real time.

01

Add Students

Create student profiles with basic details like name, roll number, class, section, and contact information.

02

Assign Class & Section

Organize students by class, section, batch, or group for easy attendance management.

03

Mark Attendance

Teachers can quickly mark students as present, absent, or late using a dynamic QR code or mobile web dashboard.

04

Admin Views Reports

School admins can track daily attendance, monthly reports, class-wise records & student attendance history.

05

Parents Get Updates

Parents receive instant updates when their child is absent, late, or attendance status is changed.
